Geroush

The ancient marshlands of Kelmeria, where endless swamps, deep forests and mysterious lakes create one of the world's most biodiverse regions.

About Geroush

Located north of Balvenos and east of Okranos, Geroush is a vast wetland region filled with ancient forests, enormous marshlands and countless lakes. Although not as harsh as the deserts of Okranos or the frozen lands of Iskaroth, Geroush remains dangerous due to the many creatures that inhabit its wilderness. The region is famous throughout Kelmeria for its incredible biodiversity, with some of the most unique ecosystems and aquatic species in the world.
